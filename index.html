WHAT IS APOCALYPSE 2029?

Apocalypse 2029 is designed to be played in the general style of a traditional tabletop roleplaying game. The Game Master describes the world, controls its dangers and inhabitants, and determines how events unfold, while the survivors are controlled by the players themselves.

There is no single predetermined story. Each World develops through play.

THE WORLD

Apocalypse 2029 takes place in a world based on the real Earth of 2029.

Cities, towns, roads, regions, buildings, governments, infrastructure, and geography may all form part of play.

The apocalypse transforms that world. Some places may remain organized. Others may collapse completely. Some communities may survive. Others may become dangerous.

The exact condition of the world depends on the World being played.

HOW THE GAME WORKS

A game of Apocalypse 2029 takes place inside a World.

A World is created by a Game Master and joined by players. Within that World, players control survivors and make decisions through roleplay.

The Game Master presents situations, locations, people, threats, and consequences. Players respond by describing what their survivors attempt to do.

The rules determine how uncertain or dangerous actions are resolved. Over time, the World changes according to what happens during play.

THE GAME MASTER

The Game Master is responsible for running the World.

  • Describe locations and events
  • Control non-player characters
  • Present dangers and opportunities
  • Decide what information players discover
  • Manage consequences
  • Create larger goals and story arcs
  • Respond to unexpected player decisions
  • Shape the state of the World

The Game Master does not simply read a predetermined story. The World is meant to respond to the players.

THE PLAYERS

Each player controls a survivor.

  • What their survivor says
  • What their survivor attempts
  • Where they go
  • Who they trust
  • What they carry
  • What risks they accept
  • What goals they pursue

Players are not expected to follow a single predetermined path. Their decisions help create the story of the World.

WORLDS

A World is an individual Apocalypse 2029 game.

Each World may have its own Game Master, players, survivors, starting location, history, major events, settlements, factions, goals, and consequences.

Different Worlds can begin in the same location and develop in completely different ways.

Creating a World does not create a match. It creates an ongoing roleplaying setting.

SURVIVORS

A survivor is a player's character within a World.

Survivors may differ in background, skills, personality, equipment, relationships, injuries, knowledge, and experience.

A survivor is more than a set of numbers. The player decides who that person is and how that person responds to the collapse of the world around them.

THE LARGER NARRATIVE

Apocalypse 2029 is not built around one universal final objective.

Instead, Worlds may develop through major goals or narrative arcs. A group may spend part of its story trying to escape an infected city. Later, its goal may become finding shelter, protecting a settlement, locating someone, dealing with a rival group, or discovering what happened elsewhere.

The larger story grows from the events of the World.

WHAT MAKES APOCALYPSE 2029 DIFFERENT?

Apocalypse 2029 is intended to combine tabletop roleplaying, survival, exploration, real-world geography, persistent Worlds, player-driven decisions, Game Master freedom, detailed locations, and long-form storytelling.

The goal is not simply to defeat zombies. The goal is to roleplay what happens when ordinary human decisions are placed inside extraordinary circumstances.
